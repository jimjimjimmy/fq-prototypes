## Feedback by Theme

### Entities Dashboard — Naming
- Naming felt unclear: "By assignee type or by item type — feels kind of necessary, but I was curious what other ideas you guys threw around"
- Didn't reject the approach, but flagged it as worth discussing alternatives

### Unexpected State / Bug
- Something triggered an unexpected deactivation on page load: "It said it was deactivated and then the page loaded"
- Unclear whether this was a bug or intended behavior — caused confusion

### Three-Way Match
- Positive reaction: "This looks cool"
- Speed concern: "Can we get this going faster?" — performance or loading time
- Loading state not clearly communicated: "It's hard to tell. I didn't know when it loaded"

### Scroll Behavior (Sidebar)
- Sidebar scroll was non-obvious — had to be in a specific zone to trigger it
- "I didn't realize I had to be over here to get it to scroll down"
- This is a discoverability/UX clarity issue

### Design Completeness
- "The design looks half done. When are we gonna be complete with all this?"
- Something specifically flagged as looking "old" — likely a legacy component still in the flow

### Documents Redesign — Header Alignment
- Documents are getting redesigned; Mike noted the header font alignment
- "The font should be either middle aligned or bottom aligned. I don't think top aligned."
- Ended on a high: "Damn, that's clean. It feels like it's worth it."

### Totals Differentiation *(Video B)*
- Grouping design complimented: "This grouping looks great"
- But totals rows need a visual differentiator — suggested options:
  - Bold
  - Gray background
  - Double underline with a line over
- "Something just to show that it's the total"

### Visual Tie-Out / Expand Behavior *(Video B)*
- When a group expands, the total line disappears — Mike wants it preserved
- "We shouldn't leave it there — leave it along with the reconciling total and also display it down here. Then you have the visual tie back up."
- Core principle: expanded state should maintain the visual connection to the total

### Filters *(Video B)*
- Filters can't scroll: "Filters can't like scroll down at all"
- Filters take up too much vertical space: "Filters gotta take up less space, way less space"
- Two separate issues: broken scroll + excessive footprint

### Expand All / Collapse All *(Video B)*
- Explicitly requested: "Expand all and collapse all makes the collapses to prepare and review"
- Needed for both prepare and review states

### Compliance App Link-Out *(Video B)*
- Clicking a link element should navigate to the compliance app with context
- "I would expect you to take me to the compliance app and I would see more context around what this is for"
- "I think we need a little more here, needs to be more helpful"
