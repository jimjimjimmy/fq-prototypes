## Why this changed

* **Terminology:** "Transform" is a separate FloQast product. Per [DATA-4115](https://floqast.atlassian.net/browse/DATA-4115) (deployed 2026-05-14), Data Studio uses "Mapping" / "Mapping Rule" instead. The new PRD's title and body have been audited accordingly — "AI-Assisted Transformation Functions" becomes "AI-Assisted Mapping Rules."

* **Q3 scope refinement (2026-05-18 / 2026-05-19):** A cross-check session against real customer SQL produced concrete scope expansions, resolved 8+ open questions, and tightened spec on functions like CONCATENATE_COLUMNS, EXTRACT_JSON_FIELD, and arithmetic operators.

* **Consolidation:** Merged with the prior "Model Creation 2 of 4: Field Mapping (DRAFT)" page since the boundary between the two documents had blurred.
