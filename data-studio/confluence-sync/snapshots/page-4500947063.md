# **1. Objective**

This sub-PRD defines the field-level specification for the Companies endpoint (POST /companies). It covers the required and optional fields of the Company data model, company hierarchy (parent-child relationships), multi-currency field handling, record versioning for lineage, submit mode behavior (append, upsert, and replace), custom field passthrough, and the metadata FloQast applies to all incoming records.

The Companies endpoint is a Phase 2 core data model endpoint in the FQ APIs initiative. It enables customers to push their organizational entity structure directly into FloQast, defining the companies that own or produce the financial data ingested via other endpoints (GL Transactions, Balances, Accounts, FX Rates). Company records provide the organizational scaffolding - including legal names, geographic context, currency designations, and intercompany hierarchies - that FloQast uses to contextualize and group financial data across entities.

Unlike transactional endpoints (GL Transactions, Balances), Companies is a low-volume, slow-changing dataset. Volume is not a primary design concern. The primary design considerations are data integrity (company records underpin nearly all other data in FloQast), hierarchy correctness (parent-child relationships must be resolvable and cycle-free), and SCD Type 2 versioning (full record history must be preserved for lineage and point-in-time auditing).

Auto-configure behavior triggered on first push is defined in the Auto-Configure sub-PRD. Upsert key design and conflict handling are defined in the Data Handling sub-PRD. Authentication is defined in the Authentication & API Key Management sub-PRD.

# **2. Key Benefits**

* Customers can push their full organizational structure into FloQast without relying on pre-built ERP connectors, enabling FQ APIs customers to establish the company reference data that all other financial data depends on.

* Separation of company_id (FloQast identifier) and source_id (source system identifier) provides traceability from FloQast back to the originating system without coupling FloQast’s internal identity model to the source system’s key structure.

* Company hierarchy support (parent-child relationships) enables FloQast to represent multi-level corporate structures, supporting intercompany workflows, rollup reporting, and elimination logic.

* Three independent currency fields (local, functional, reporting) capture the full multi-currency context for each company, giving FloQast the information needed to apply the correct exchange rate type for each translation step.

* SCD Type 2 versioning via effective_start_date and effective_end_date preserves the full history of company record changes, enabling point-in-time reconstruction of the company structure as it existed on any past date.

* Active flag allows historical companies to remain in FloQast for continuity and reporting while making clear they are no longer operationally relevant, without requiring deletion of their records.

* Entity association links companies to FloQast entities, making the relationship between source-system companies and FloQast’s internal entity model explicit and queryable.

# **3. Use Cases**

1. A customer pushes their full company list on initial setup, including all required fields and optional currency and geographic fields. FloQast ingests the records and makes the company structure available for entity association and downstream financial data workflows.

2. A customer submits a parent-child company hierarchy, with subsidiary companies referencing their parent via parent_source_id. FloQast resolves the hierarchy and stores the relationships, enabling intercompany and consolidation workflows.

3. A customer acquires a new subsidiary. They push the new company record in upsert mode. FloQast inserts the new company and leaves all existing records unchanged.

4. A customer’s subsidiary changes its functional currency following a restructuring. They push the updated company record with the new functional_currency value in upsert mode. FloQast updates the matching record using source_id as the key, sets the previous version’s effective_end_date, and creates a new version with the updated currency and a new effective_start_date.

5. A customer decommissions a subsidiary. They push the updated record with active = false in upsert mode. The company remains in FloQast for historical reporting but is flagged as inactive.

6. A customer replaces their full company list after a corporate reorganization. They push all active and historical companies in replace mode. FloQast removes all existing company records for the customer and replaces them with the re-submitted set.

# **4. Success Metrics**

* Companies endpoint available and functional at Phase 2 launch.

* Required field validation correctly rejecting payloads with missing required fields, with actionable error messages identifying the missing fields and affected records (keyed to source_id).

* Company hierarchy correctly stored: parent-child relationships resolvable, cycle detection preventing circular hierarchies.

* Upsert correctly matching on source_id and updating existing records without creating duplicates.

* SCD Type 2 versioning correctly applied: effective_end_date set on the previous version when a record is updated via upsert.

* Currency fields validated against FloQast’s currency table; invalid codes rejected with clear error messages.

* FloQast metadata accurately applied to all ingested company records (target accuracy: 100%).

# **5. Assumptions**

* Customers are responsible for pre-formatting company data to match the field specifications defined in this document. FloQast does not perform transformation or normalization.

* submit_mode is required on every push. There is no default mode - customers must explicitly specify append, upsert, or replace.

* In append mode, all submitted records are inserted as new rows unconditionally. No key matching is performed. Submitting the same record twice across pushes creates duplicate rows.

* In upsert mode, records are matched using source_id as the upsert key. Matching records are updated; non-matching records are inserted. Records not in the batch are left unchanged. Upsert is idempotent. When a record is updated via upsert, the system advances the record version: the previous record’s effective_end_date is set, and the updated record becomes the new current version.

* In replace mode, all existing company records are removed before the submitted batch is inserted. Replace mode for Companies is not entity- or period-scoped - it replaces all company records for the customer. See Open Questions for scope clarification.

* source_id is the customer-supplied unique identifier for the company in the originating system. It is the upsert key and the identifier used in error reports. It must be unique within the customer’s dataset.

* company_id is the FloQast-assigned internal identifier for the company record. It is system-generated and not customer-supplied. Customers reference companies using source_id; FloQast maps source_id to company_id internally. See Open Questions.

* Company hierarchy is expressed by including parent_source_id on child records, referencing the source_id of the parent company. FloQast resolves the hierarchy after ingestion. Circular references (company A is parent of B; B is parent of A) are rejected. Orphaned parent references (parent_source_id references a source_id not present in the dataset) are flagged as warnings or errors - TBD with Engineering (see Open Questions).

* local_currency, functional_currency, and reporting_currency are optional. When provided, values must match ISO 4217 currency codes recognized in FloQast’s currency table. Invalid codes are rejected.

* entity_association links the company to one or more FloQast entities. If a company maps to multiple FloQast entities, the payload mechanism for expressing this relationship (array of entity IDs vs. separate records) is TBD - see Open Questions.

* effective_start_date and effective_end_date follow the same SCD Type 2 versioning pattern used in the FX Rates sub-PRD: null effective_end_date means this is the currently active version; a non-null value means the version has been superseded.

* Companies is a low-volume, slow-changing dataset. Typical customers will have hundreds to low thousands of company records. High-volume batch session infrastructure is not expected to be required for this endpoint.

* System-applied metadata fields (touch_count and FloQast ingestion timestamp) are generated by FloQast at ingestion time and cannot be overridden.

# **8. Requirements**

**REQ-CO-01: As a customer technical team, I want to submit the required set of Company fields so that FloQast can correctly ingest and represent the organizational structure that underlies all financial data.**

***Functional spec:** The Companies endpoint accepts the required fields defined in Section 7. Every record must include all required fields. Records with missing required fields are rejected with a 400 Bad Request response identifying the specific missing fields and affected records (keyed to source_id where available). source_id must be unique within the customer’s dataset - duplicate source_id values within a single append batch are rejected. active must be a valid boolean value. Required field values must conform to the specified data types defined in Section 7.*

**REQ-CO-02: As a customer technical team, I want to include optional Company fields in my payload so that I can provide richer organizational context where available without being required to populate every field.**

***Functional spec:** The Companies endpoint accepts the optional fields defined in Section 7. Optional fields may be included or omitted on a per-record basis. When included, they must conform to the specified data types; type or value mismatches return a 422 error identifying the offending field and record. Currency fields (local_currency, functional_currency, reporting_currency), when provided, must match ISO 4217 codes recognized in FloQast’s currency table - unrecognized codes are rejected with a 400 error. When optional fields are omitted, the field is treated as absent and no error is raised. Optional fields present in the payload are captured by auto-configure on first push per the Auto-Configure sub-PRD.*

**REQ-CO-03: As a customer technical team, I want to specify whether my submission should append to, upsert into, or replace the existing company data so that I have explicit control over how multiple submissions are handled.**

***Functional spec:** Each push must include a submit_mode parameter: append, upsert, or replace. Append: all submitted records are inserted unconditionally. No key matching is performed. Submitting the same source_id twice creates duplicate rows. Upsert: records are matched on source_id. Matching records are updated; non-matching records are inserted. Records not in the batch are left unchanged. Idempotent. When a record is updated via upsert, FloQast advances the SCD Type 2 version: the previous record’s effective_end_date is set to the current date and the updated record becomes the new active version. Replace: all existing company records for the customer are removed before the submitted batch is inserted. See Open Questions for scope clarification. submit_mode is required on every push - requests without it are rejected with a 400 error.*

**REQ-CO-04: As a customer technical team, I want to establish parent-child relationships between companies so that FloQast can represent multi-level corporate hierarchies for intercompany workflows and consolidation.**

***Functional spec:** Customers establish company hierarchy by including parent_source_id on child company records, referencing the source_id of the parent. FloQast resolves the hierarchy after ingestion. The following rules apply: circular references (where a chain of parent_source_id values leads back to the starting company) are rejected with a 400 error identifying the cycle. Parent references to a source_id not present in FloQast (neither previously ingested nor in the current batch) are flagged - handling (reject vs. accept with warning) is TBD with Engineering (see Open Questions). Top-level companies (no parent) omit parent_source_id or submit it as null. There is no defined limit on hierarchy depth for Phase 2, but Engineering should assess performance implications for very deep trees.*

**REQ-CO-05: As a customer technical team, I want to include fields in my Companies payload that are not part of the standard model so that I can pass non-standard company attributes through to FloQast without being rejected.**

***Functional spec:** Fields present in a push payload that are not defined as required or optional in the standard Companies model are treated as custom fields. The API accepts records containing custom fields without error. Custom fields are passed through as-is. On first push, auto-configure detects and registers custom fields in the lineage per the Auto-Configure sub-PRD.*

**REQ-CO-06: As a FloQast system, I want to apply standard metadata to all Company records received via the API so that submissions are traceable, auditable, and correctly attributed to their source.**

***Functional spec:** FloQast applies the following metadata to each Company record at ingestion. These fields are system-generated and cannot be overridden: company_id (FloQast-assigned unique identifier for the company record, generated at first ingestion), fq_created_at (timestamp of initial ingestion into FloQast), fq_ingested_by (connection and API key identifier associated with the push that created the record), touch_count (number of times the record has been updated via subsequent pushes, starting at 1 on first ingestion). Metadata fields are visible within FloQast for reporting and audit purposes.*

# **9. User Interaction and Design**

The Companies endpoint is a developer-facing API. There is no UI component - customers interact with it programmatically through their integration code or API tooling.

## **Endpoint**

POST /companies

## **Authentication**

API key passed in the Authorization: Bearer header, consistent with all other FQ API endpoints. See Authentication & API Key Management sub-PRD.

## **Request Structure**

The request body is a JSON object with the following top-level parameters:

* submit_mode (required): “append”, “upsert”, or “replace”.

* records (required): array of Company objects, each conforming to the field specification in Section 7.

Note: unlike GL Transactions and Balances, Companies does not take a period or entity_id parameter at the top level. Company records are not period-scoped. Entity association is expressed per record via the entity_association field.

## **Submit Mode Behavior**

| **submit_mode** | **Behavior** | **When to Use** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **append** | All submitted records inserted unconditionally. No key matching. Duplicate source_id values across pushes create duplicate rows. | New company records with no overlap with existing data. Not idempotent. |
| **upsert** | Records matched on source_id. Matches updated; non-matches inserted. Unsubmitted records unchanged. SCD Type 2 version advanced on update. Idempotent. | Incremental updates: adding companies, changing active status, updating names or currencies, revising hierarchy. The default mode for ongoing company maintenance. |
| **replace** | All existing company records within the defined scope (TBD - see Open Questions) are removed before the submitted batch is inserted. | Full company list refresh after a major reorganization. Use when the authoritative source re-generates the complete company structure. |

## **Company Hierarchy**

Company hierarchy is expressed inline within the record payload using the parent_source_id field. A child company includes the source_id of its parent in parent_source_id. FloQast resolves the full hierarchy tree after each push. There is no separate hierarchy endpoint.

Ingestion order within a batch does not need to match hierarchy order - FloQast resolves parent references after all records in the batch are staged. Parent references to companies ingested in prior pushes are also resolved correctly. Only circular references and unresolvable orphaned references are rejected (see Section 10 for orphan handling).

## **SCD Type 2 Versioning**

Company records follow a Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2 (SCD2) versioning model. Each change to a company record creates a new version rather than overwriting the previous one, preserving the full history of the record.

When a company record is updated via upsert:

1. FloQast identifies the current active version of the record (where effective_end_date is null).

2. The current version’s effective_end_date is set to the date of the update.

3. The submitted record becomes the new active version, with a null effective_end_date (or the customer-supplied effective_start_date and effective_end_date if provided).

4. touch_count is incremented.

Customers may also supply effective_start_date and effective_end_date explicitly in the payload to control versioning from the source system’s perspective. When supplied, FloQast uses the customer-provided values rather than deriving them from the ingestion timestamp.

## **Error Responses**

* 400 Bad Request: missing required fields, missing or invalid submit_mode, duplicate source_id within an append batch, circular parent reference, unrecognized currency code, malformed payload.

* 401 Unauthorized: API key missing, invalid, or revoked.

* 403 Forbidden: valid key used outside its connection scope.

* 422 Unprocessable Entity: field value present but wrong data type or outside allowed values.

* 429 Too Many Requests: rate limit exceeded. Includes Retry-After header.

* 500 Internal Server Error: unexpected system failure.

| **Question** | **Owner** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is company_id FloQast-generated or customer-supplied? | PM / Engineering | Current assumption: company_id is system-generated by FloQast; customers reference companies using source_id. If customers need to supply company_id (e.g., to align with an existing FQ entity ID), this changes the field’s category and may affect the upsert key. Confirm before implementation. |
| How is entity_association expressed when a company maps to multiple FloQast entities? | PM / Engineering | A company may be associated with more than one FQ entity (e.g., a holding company with subsidiaries across multiple entities). Options: (1) array of entity IDs on the record; (2) one record per entity association (source_id + entity_id as the composite key). Decision affects both payload structure and the upsert key definition. |
| What is the scope of replace mode for Companies - all companies globally, or constrained to a subset? | PM / Engineering | Unlike GL Transactions and Balances, Companies is not naturally period- or entity-scoped. A full replace would wipe and replace all company records for the customer, which is high-risk. Consider constraining replace to a subset defined by entity_association or by the set of source_ids in the submitted batch. Recommend discussing with Engineering before implementation. |
| How should orphaned parent references be handled - reject the record or accept with a warning? | Engineering / PM | An orphaned parent reference occurs when parent_source_id references a source_id not found in FloQast. Strict validation (reject) prevents incomplete hierarchies but blocks ingestion if the parent hasn’t been loaded yet. Lenient validation (accept with a warning flag) is more forgiving but may leave unresolved hierarchy nodes. Recommend strict validation with a clear error message, as companies are low-volume and should be loaded completely. |
| Should country and state/province values be validated against a reference list, or accepted as free-text? | Engineering / PM | ISO 3166 codes provide consistency and enable geographic grouping, but some customers may use non-standard values from their ERPs. Options: (1) validate against ISO 3166-1 (country) and ISO 3166-2 (state/province); (2) accept free-text with a recommendation to use ISO codes. Recommend validation for country in Phase 1 and regional validation in Phase 2 to ensure data quality for downstream grouping. |
| Should SCD Type 2 version advancement (setting effective_end_date on the previous version) happen automatically on upsert, or should customers supply effective_start_date and effective_end_date explicitly? | PM / Engineering | Current assumption: FloQast automatically advances the version on upsert, but customers may also supply explicit effective dates to control versioning from the source system. Engineering to confirm whether both approaches are supported and how conflicts (customer supplies effective_start_date that contradicts the existing record) are handled. |
| What is the unique record identifier used in error reports for the Companies endpoint in the Ingestion Logging & Status sub-PRD? | PM / Engineering | Recommendation: source_id. This is the customer-supplied key and should be consistent with how the Logging sub-PRD references the unique identifier for this endpoint. Confirm with Engineering. |
