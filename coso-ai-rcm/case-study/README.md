# Case Study: Coso Ai Rcm

**Status:** exploration  
**Jira:** _Not tracked._

---

## Problem

Product experience for FloQast's COSO-aligned AI Risk and Control Matrix (RCM) - the compliance program around AI agents used in financial close. Structured around three lines of defense: Accounting Manager (1st), IT/Compliance Manager (2nd), Internal/External Auditor (3rd).

---

## Goal

A command center for AI governance that turns FloQast's AI activity into auditable, capability-mapped evidence without the accounting team doing extra work.
