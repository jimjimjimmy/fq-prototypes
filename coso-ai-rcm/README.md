# COSO AI RCM

> Designing the AI compliance experience inside FloQast — agent inventory, risk assessment, controls, monitoring, and evidence — across three personas.

## What This Is

FloQast's AI features (Transform, AI Matching, AI Accruals, etc.) need a compliance layer that satisfies COSO and SOX requirements for AI-driven controls. This project explores what that experience looks like for the three people involved: the accounting manager who runs the agent, the compliance manager who governs the program, and the auditor who validates it.

The immediate focus is **Persona 2 — the IT/Compliance Manager** (second line of defense): the person who maintains the agent inventory, facilitates risk assessments, designs controls, sets up recurring tasks, and tracks gaps to closure.
